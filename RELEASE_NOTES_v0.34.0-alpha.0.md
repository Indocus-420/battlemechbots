# BattleMech Foundry System 0.34.0-alpha.0

Final alpha test-play build for Foundry VTT 14.363 and newer.

## Test-play readiness

- Includes 95 BattleMechs organized into Light, Medium, Heavy, and Assault actor folders.
- Includes 23 deployment-ready combat vehicles.
- Includes the Raven RVN-1X with its required equipment and presentation media.
- Weapon catalog entries have packaged thumbnails, firing visuals, impact visuals, and audio fallbacks.
- Mechs and vehicles have activation artwork and audio presentation profiles.
- Includes movement, firing, physical attacks, heat, ammunition, damage, criticals, initiative, GATOR, Resolve, pilot abilities, firing arcs, radar, terrain, fog, and token vision systems.

## Raven X-1 electronic warfare

- X-1 EW Equipment is recognized as a prototype Active Probe and ECM suite.
- The Systems hotbar exposes **X-1 Area Sensor Lock** when the suite is operational.
- The action identifies enemy contacts within six hexes.
- Affected targets lose two points of movement evasion, to a minimum of zero.
- Sensor Lock and ECM state are recorded for the current round.
- Using the action consumes the Raven's firing action.

## Verification

- Full automated suite: 208 passing tests, 0 failures.
- Actor and item media paths were visually audited in the running GIL 2 world.
- All packaged BattleMechs and vehicles pass deployment validation.

This remains an alpha test build. Use a duplicate or backup world for controlled playtesting and record rules discrepancies encountered during play.
