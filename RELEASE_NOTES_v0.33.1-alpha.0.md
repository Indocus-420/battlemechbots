# BattleMech Foundry System 0.33.1-alpha.0

This controlled-test update adds the missing Raven from the official HBS Urban Warfare design-data source.

- Adds Raven RVN-1X to the Light Mechs compendium with authentic 35-ton structure, armor distribution, 5/8 movement, two Medium Lasers, SRM 6 and ammunition, and 12 heat sinks.
- Adds X-1 EW Equipment as a reusable equipment item with its 7.5-ton construction mass, four critical slots, ECM-field description, and Active Probe area Sensor Lock description.
- Marks ECM stealth charges, indirect-fire immunity, and area Sensor Lock as manual adjudication until dedicated electronic-warfare automation is implemented.
- Adds original Raven portrait and activation audio assets; no HBS artwork or audio is redistributed.
- Preserves the experimental RVN-3X and prototype TSM source discovery for a later rules increment instead of presenting unimplemented TSM behavior as deployment-ready.

Validation: 207 automated tests pass, including deployment mass, ammunition compatibility, critical-slot collision, media integrity, compendium synchronization, and storefront coverage.
